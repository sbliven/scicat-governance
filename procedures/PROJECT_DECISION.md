# Project Decisions

## Abbreviations and Links

Project = [SciCat Project](../project-components/SCICAT-PROJECT.md)
PL = [Project Leaders](../governance-entities/PROJECT-LEADERS.md)
SC = [Steering Committee](../governance-entities/STEERING-COMMITTEE.md)

## Description

The Project Leaders (PL) are jointly responsible for decisions affecting the Project.

## Major Decisions

For decisions with significant impact on the Project or its community, the PL will determine suitable steps to achieve community consensus before deciding. This may include:

- discussion at contributor meetings,
- a formal vote following the [Voting](./VOTING.md) procedure, or
- consultation with the Steering Committee (SC).

The PL will publicly announce major decisions and the process used to reach them through the official project channels.

## SC Oversight

The SC may choose to override a PL decision or request additional actions. Any SC member may request a meeting of the SC to review a PL decision.
